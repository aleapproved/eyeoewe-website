Eye O Ewe is an app for keeping track of shared expenses in a group. This policy explains what information the app and its service handle, why, who else is involved, how long it’s kept and what your rights are.

Eye O Ewe is run by [CONTROLLER NAME] (“we”), who is responsible for your information under UK data protection law. You can contact us about anything in this policy at privacy@eyeoewe.app.

What we can’t see

Your groups are end-to-end encrypted. Group names, the names of the people in them, and every expense and payment (amounts, descriptions, categories) are encrypted on your phone before they reach our servers, and only the people in the group can read them. We can’t read them, and neither can anyone we work with. Your password and recovery words never leave your phone at all.

What encryption doesn’t hide: which account is in which group, when things were added or changed, and how big they are. Everyone in a group can see its whole history, including what was added before they joined, and anyone who leaves keeps what they could already see.

Other members may add your name, and expenses and payments that involve you, to a group. These are encrypted in the same way, so we can’t read them either.

What we handle, and why

  • Your email address. To create your account, send you codes to confirm your email when you create an account or reset your password, and tell you when your password changes. We don’t send marketing emails.
  • Your password. It never leaves your phone. Your phone turns it into a sign-in credential and an encryption key; our servers store only a scrambled (hashed) form of the credential.
  • Account and group records. A random account ID, which groups you’re in and your role in them, and records of invitations. We need these to run the service. The secret part of an invitation link never reaches our website or servers.
  • Your phone’s notification token, if you turn notifications on. To send you notifications about your groups. When a new expense or payment is added, the notification may carry that entry still encrypted, so your phone can show what was added; our servers and the notification services can’t read it.
  • Subscription records, if you open the Pro page or subscribe. A random billing ID (not your email or account ID), whether you have Pro and until when, and the store’s purchase records. To give you Pro and keep it in step with your subscription.
  • Security and usage limits. Counts of how often an account adds things and of wrong password attempts, to stop abuse, and a short record when someone without Pro reaches the group limit, so we can see whether the limit works.
  • Support emails. If you contact us through Get in touch, we get your email address and whatever you write. If you choose to attach a diagnostic report, it holds information such as your app version, phone make and model, phone system version, text size, time zone, and a list of recent technical event and error codes. It never holds your groups, names, amounts or any of your encrypted information. Your phone keeps this log for up to 7 days, and it’s only sent if you attach it.

You need an email address and a password to use Eye O Ewe; without them we can’t create your account. Everything else is optional. We don’t make decisions about you by automated means.

We don’t use advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services, and we don’t sell your information. Our website sets no cookies; it remembers your light or dark choice in your browser only.

Our legal reasons

  • To do what you asked us to (providing the service): your account, email address, sign-in, groups, notifications and Pro.
  • Our legitimate interests: keeping the service secure and preventing abuse (usage limits and limiting password guesses), checking that the group limit works, and answering support emails and diagnostic reports you choose to send. We use as little information as we can for each.
  • Legal obligations: where the law requires us to keep or share information.

Who else is involved

We use these companies to run Eye O Ewe. Each gets only what it needs:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database, sign-in and server functions in [REGION]. It holds your account and group records, your encrypted groups, notification tokens, subscription status and usage records, and sees your internet address when the app connects.
  • Resend, which sends our emails. It gets your email address and the email’s text.
  • Expo and Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging), which deliver notifications. They get your notification token, your account ID, random IDs for the group, a general message such as “New expense”, and, for a new entry, that entry still encrypted.
  • RevenueCat, which manages subscriptions. It gets your random billing ID, your purchase records, your app version, phone model and system version, and your internet (IP) address.
  • Google Play and, on iPhone, Apple’s App Store, which take payment. They handle your payment details themselves; we never see them.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts our website. Like any website host, it sees the internet address of people who visit it.
  • [MAIL PROVIDER], which hosts our inbox. It holds emails you send us.

How long we keep it

  • Your account information is kept until you delete your account.
  • To delete your account, first leave or delete each of your groups. We then delete your email address, password record, notification tokens, recovery information, subscription and group-limit records straight away, and ask RevenueCat to delete its record. We keep only a record that a deleted account ID existed. Expenses, payments and your name as written in groups you were in stay, encrypted, in those groups’ history and on members’ phones.
  • Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play.
  • Our backup copy may hold your information until it’s replaced at the next backup. Supabase may keep its own sign-in logs for the period in its policy.
  • Notification tokens are removed when you turn notifications off, sign out or delete your account. Usage counts are cleared within a day. Subscription event records are deleted after 90 days, group-limit records after 30 days and records of password-change emails after 30 days. Personal invitations expire after 7 days; group invitation links work until an admin resets them. We keep support emails for [PERIOD].
  • Our providers keep their own technical logs, such as internet addresses, for the periods in their own policies.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, to delete it, to receive it in a form you can reuse, or to restrict how we use it. We can’t read your encrypted groups, so a copy we send includes only what we hold in readable form; your groups’ contents are in the app.

You can object at any time to our use of your information for our legitimate interests.

Email privacy@eyeoewe.app and we’ll reply within one month.

You can delete your account in the app (account menu › Delete account) or at https://eyeoewe.app/eyeoewe/v1/delete-account/.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
