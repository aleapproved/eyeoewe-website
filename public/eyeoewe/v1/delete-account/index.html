Eye O Ewe

Delete your Eye O Ewe account

In the app

Account menu › Delete account. First leave or delete each of your groups.

Without the app

Email privacy@eyeoewe.app from the address you signed up with and say you want your account deleted. We’ll confirm and delete it within one month.

What’s deleted and what’s kept

  • We delete your email address, password record, notification tokens, recovery information and subscription records straight away, and ask RevenueCat to delete its record. We keep only a record that a deleted account ID existed. Expenses, payments and your name as written in groups you were in stay, encrypted, in those groups’ history and on members’ phones.
  • Our backup copy may hold your information until it’s replaced at the next backup. Supabase may keep its own sign-in logs for the period in its policy.

More detail is in our privacy policy.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a Google Play subscription: cancel it in Google Play.
