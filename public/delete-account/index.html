Delete your Eye O Ewe account

Eye O Ewe is made by aleapproved. You can delete your account in the app, or without it by email.

In the app

  1. Leave or delete each of your groups. Where you’re the only admin of a group with other people in it, make someone else an admin first, or delete the group.
  2. Tap your profile circle at the top right, then Delete account.
  3. Tap Permanently delete account.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email privacy@eyeoewe.app from the address you signed up with and say you want your account deleted. We’ll reply to that address to confirm, then delete your account within one month. If you’re still in any groups, we’ll take you out of them first. A group where you’re the only member is deleted; where you’re the only admin, another member becomes admin.

What’s deleted

When your account is deleted, we delete at once: your email address, password record, profile, encryption keys, pending invitations, notification tokens and recovery information.

What’s kept

  • Your random account ID, as a record that it existed and of which groups it was in and what it added.
  • Expenses, payments and your name as written in groups you were in stay, encrypted, in those groups’ history and on members’ phones, so the other members’ balances stay right. We can’t read them.
  • Our daily backups keep your information for up to 7 days, and the copy taken before each update that changes our database keeps it until the next such update.
  • Supabase, which runs our sign-in, keeps a log of sign-ins and account changes, which can include your email and internet address; deleting your account doesn’t delete it. It and our other providers also keep their own technical logs for the periods in their own policies.
  • Emails you sent us are kept for 2 years after the conversation ends.
  • Eye O Ewe’s saved data stays on any other phone you’re signed in on until you remove the app there.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
