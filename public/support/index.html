From the app

If something went wrong, the easiest way is from inside Eye O Ewe:

  1. Tap your profile circle at the top right.
  2. Tap Get in touch.
  3. Add a subject and your message. To include the diagnostic report, tick Attach diagnostic report. Tap Open email app, then send the email from there.

The diagnostic report holds your app version, phone make and model, Android version, text size and time zone, and the app’s recent technical events and error codes. It never holds names, amounts, recovery words or anything from your groups.

Common questions

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? To set a new one, you’ll need your email and your recovery words. Without recovery words your groups can’t be unlocked with a new password, by you or by us: that’s what end-to-end encryption means. If you’ve lost both your password and your recovery words, create a new account and ask an admin of each group to invite you again. To use the same email address for it, first ask us to delete your old account.
